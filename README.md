Bazarnote is a **simple, offline shopping list app** built for minimalism and speed.
Stay organized while shopping—no cloud, no tracking, just your list.

## 🛒 What is it?

Bazarnote is a no-fuss shopping list app designed to be:

- ✅ Simple — Just open, add items, and shop.
- 📶 Offline-first — Works without internet.
- 🔐 Private — No data collection or syncing.
- 🛍️ Focused — Designed specifically for bazaar-style and regular grocery shopping.

With Bazarnote, you can quickly jot down items, keep track of your products and focus on shopping.
