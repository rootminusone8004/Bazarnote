* Bug Report

  - checkboxes should change their states anytime they are pressed

** Actual Behaviour
  - checkbox doesn't update the first time when they are pressed

** Steps to Reproduce
  1. Open the app.
  2. Go to the main page.
  3. Create a new note. (Existing note will also work.)
  4. Toggle the checkbox.
  5. Add price and save.
